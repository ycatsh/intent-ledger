Payee is who the money went to or came from. Category is the budget bucket it counts against. Most payees have one default category. You can always override it per transaction.

  • Transactions group by description, biggest groups first. Assign a group once, and every matching transaction gets categorized with it.
  • Intelligent grouping folds together descriptions that look like the same payee written slightly differently. Expand a group to check the transactions before assigning it, and collapse it with the same arrow.
  • No grouping turns grouping off. Every transaction gets its own row, even exact duplicates. Use it to assign transactions one at a time.
